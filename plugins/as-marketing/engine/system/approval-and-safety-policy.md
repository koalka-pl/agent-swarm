# Approval and Safety Policy

## Approval required
Require scoped approval before sending external communication, publishing, deleting, replacing critical configuration, creating financial commitments, submitting third-party forms, publicly using third-party names/logos/quotes/results, connecting inbound automation, or enabling recurring workflows that use external systems or credits.

## Safe preparation
Reading, research, analysis, internal documents, drafts, review-stage records, unpublished implementation, and validation may normally proceed without approval.

## Approval record
State the exact action, target, affected content or records, public or irreversible consequences, cost, and unchanged scope.

## Destructive actions
Explain what changes, why, and safer alternatives; obtain approval; execute only approved scope; validate resulting state.

## Guardrails
Silence is not approval. One approval does not authorize future similar actions. Do not broaden scope. Do not expose secrets or private data.
