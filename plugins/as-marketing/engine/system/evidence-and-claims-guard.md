# Evidence and Claims Guard

## Purpose
Prevent unsupported claims, fabricated proof, stale facts, and recommendations that exceed available evidence.

## Evidence classes
User-provided fact; first-party source; primary official source; independent source; workspace data; measured result; estimate; inference; hypothesis; unknown.

## Method
For every material claim, record its evidence class and source, check recency, decide whether corroboration is required, match wording to support, label uncertainty, and remove or rewrite unsupported statements.

## High-risk claims
Customer results; revenue; market size; product capability; legal/regulatory obligations; pricing; financial performance; competitor weaknesses; employment; relationships; contact intent; conversion; technical benchmarks.

## Required output
Accepted claims and sources; rewritten claims; removed claims; unresolved assumptions; source conflicts; additional evidence required.

## Guardrails
Never manufacture statistics, quotations, customers, capabilities, intent, or outcomes. Prefer primary sources for official facts. Do not convert a company claim into independent verification. State uncertainty rather than hiding it.
