# Acquisition & Distribution Router

## Purpose
Select exactly one owner skill for non-paid customer acquisition, demand creation, distribution, partnerships, events, referrals, community, or targeted outreach.

## Owns
Routing only. It does not execute channel strategy itself.

## Required context
Business objective, ICP, offer, buying stage, current constraint, available proof, channels, relationships, capacity, budget, time horizon, desired action, and available measurement.

## Routing
- Create awareness and preference before active demand → Demand Generation Strategist
- Produce identifiable qualified inquiries → Lead Generation Strategist
- Build selective expert-led manual outreach → Founder-Led Outbound Strategist
- Coordinate activity for named strategic accounts → Account-Based Marketing Strategist
- Grow through complementary organizations or ecosystems → Partner-Led Growth Strategist
- Build trust through sustained community participation → Community Growth Strategist
- Turn events into learning, meetings, and opportunities → Event Marketing Strategist
- Design a repeatable introduction mechanism → Referral Program Designer
- Distribute expertise and create conversations on organic social → Organic Social Distribution Strategist

## Selection rules
1. Route from the immediate decision, not the channel named casually.
2. Choose one owner skill.
3. Load at most two supporting specialists only when necessary.
4. If ICP, positioning, or offer is unresolved, route upstream before acquisition planning.
5. If the bottleneck is unknown, diagnose the growth constraint first.
6. Exclude paid-media planning and execution.

## Common ambiguities
- Demand generation changes beliefs; lead generation captures identifiable responses.
- Founder-led outbound targets people directly; ABM coordinates a multi-role named-account motion.
- Organic social distributes expertise; community growth follows community norms and recurring contribution.
- Partner-led growth is broader than referrals and requires mutual operating value.

## Output
Selected owner skill, routing rationale, required inputs, evidence gaps, excluded alternatives, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not route to more channels as a substitute for prioritization. Do not infer intent from engagement. Do not include Paid Acquisition Router. External messages, posts, and partner commitments remain approval-gated.
