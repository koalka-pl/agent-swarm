# Content, SEO & AEO Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for content strategy, campaigns, briefs, repurposing, organic search, AI visibility, channel strategy, lifecycle content, or content performance.

## Required context
Business goal, audience, product/offer, positioning, customer questions, existing content, proof, channels, search data, capacity, conversion path, and measurement.

## Routing
- Define what content should exist and why → Content Strategy Architect
- Sequence a bounded campaign and editorial cadence → Editorial Campaign Planner
- Prepare one evidence-ready asset brief → Content Brief Architect
- Turn one source into channel-native derivatives → Content Repurposing Strategist
- Design organic-search strategy from audience and product value → SEO Strategy Architect
- Research and prioritize queries by intent and impact → Keyword and Intent Researcher
- Organize content into pillars, clusters, and links → Topic Cluster Architect
- Improve an existing page for matched search intent → On-Page SEO Optimizer
- Improve retrieval, citation, mention, and recommendation in AI answers → AEO and AI Visibility Strategist
- Plan LinkedIn’s strategic role and distribution system → LinkedIn Content Strategist
- Plan recurring newsletter or lifecycle communication → Newsletter and Lifecycle Content Strategist
- Evaluate content outcomes and portfolio decisions → Content Performance Analyst

## Rules
Choose one owner and at most two specialists. Apply Audience → Product → Topics → Keywords. Strategy and briefs precede writing. Preserve evidence and source traceability. Route technical crawl/indexing work outside this pack.

## Output
Selected owner, rationale, required inputs, evidence gaps, excluded routes, dependencies, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not start from volume alone, create thin pages, invent keyword data, guarantee rankings/citations, or treat publishing as distribution.
