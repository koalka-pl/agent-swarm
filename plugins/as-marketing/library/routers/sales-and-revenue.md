# Sales & Revenue Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for sales process, discovery, proposals, pipeline, CRM, revenue handoffs, customer success, expansion, or pilot transition.

## Required context
GTM motion, offer, ICP, sales cycle, opportunity stage, stakeholders, current process, systems, ownership, evidence, and desired decision.

## Routing
- Map assets and proof to sales stages → Sales Enablement Strategist
- Build an expert-led founder sales process → Founder-Led Sales Planner
- Standardize discovery and qualification → Discovery and Qualification Architect
- Design contextual outreach follow-up → Outbound Sequence Designer
- Translate discovery into scope and internal justification → Proposal and Business Case Writer
- Define opportunity stages and review discipline → Pipeline Process Designer
- Design CRM objects, fields, relationships, and governance → CRM Architecture Strategist
- Align lifecycle definitions and team handoffs → Revenue Operations Architect
- Prioritize leads by fit and behavior → Lead Scoring Designer
- Assign leads and define fallbacks/SLAs → Lead Routing Designer
- Build onboarding, adoption, and value realization → Customer Success Strategist
- Plan appropriate renewal or expansion → Expansion and Renewal Planner
- Turn pilot evidence into a production decision → Pilot-to-Implementation Planner

## Rules
Choose one owner; load at most two specialists. Define process before automation. Pipeline stages represent buyer progress. Use one system of record. Route offer, pricing, positioning, or acquisition questions upstream.

## Output
Selected owner, rationale, inputs, evidence gaps, excluded routes, dependencies, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not invent budget, authority, intent, value, or forecast certainty. External outreach and commercial commitments require approval.
