# Positioning & Product Marketing Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for positioning, messaging, value propositions, category framing, brand strategy, product marketing, use cases, launches, thought leadership, or proof architecture.

## Required context
Audience evidence, jobs and triggers, alternatives, offer, capabilities, proof, company stage, buying process, and desired customer action.

## Routing
- What should the company or offer be known for? → Positioning Strategist
- Inconsistent communication across audiences or channels → Messaging Architect
- Customer value is difficult to explain → Value Proposition Designer
- Compete in, redefine, or create a category → Category Design Strategist
- Brand promise, narrative, voice, or principles → Brand Strategy Architect
- Segments, use cases, adoption, enablement, or product launch → Product Marketing Strategist
- Select and structure the strongest use case → Use Case Strategist
- Coordinate a launch sequence → Launch Strategy Planner
- Build evidence-backed expert authority → Thought Leadership Strategist
- Match claims to proof and identify credibility gaps → Proof and Credibility Architect

## Availability
All routes in this router have standalone full skill definitions.

## Selection rules
Choose one owner. Positioning precedes messaging; customer value precedes copy; proof requirements are explicit. Route weak customer evidence upstream and pricing questions to Offer/Pricing.

## Excludes
Detailed copywriting, website implementation, broad market research, and pricing.

## Output
Selected owner, strategic question, required evidence, downstream implications, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not invent differentiation, category demand, customer value, proof, or authority.
