# Offer, Pricing & Monetization Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for offer structure, paid pilots, pricing, packaging, economics, scope risk, monetization tests, productization, and offer ladders.

## Required context
Target customer, trigger, verified problem, desired outcome, current alternative, delivery process, variable scope, costs, risks, proof, buying process, and expansion path.

## Routing
- Customer and problem are known but offer is unclear → Offer Architecture Strategist
- Design a bounded commercial validation engagement → Paid Pilot Designer
- Price, package, value metric, fences, or commercial terms → Pricing and Packaging Strategist
- Package bespoke expertise into repeatable delivery → Service Productization Architect
- Evaluate margin, CAC, payback, retention, or contribution → Unit Economics Analyst
- Diagnose consulting or service-delivery profitability → Service Economics Auditor
- Test a revenue mechanism → Monetization Experiment Designer
- Analyze scope creep, uncertainty, and delivery dependencies → Scope and Delivery Risk Analyst
- Create a sequence from entry offer to expansion → Offer Ladder Designer
- Evidence suggests the offer should change → Offer Evolution Planner

## Availability
All routes have standalone definitions. Service Productization Architect and Offer Evolution Planner are registered from `skills/core/` and resolve through the active fail-closed runtime.

## Selection rules
Choose one owner. Route general model selection to Business Model Strategist. Resolve customer/problem evidence before packaging. Separate price strategy from delivery economics when both require deep work.

## Excludes
General business-model selection, final sales proposals, campaign distribution, and implementation delivery.

## Output
Selected owner, commercial decision, required evidence, assumptions, risk, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not invent willingness to pay, margins, costs, scope certainty, or customer value.
