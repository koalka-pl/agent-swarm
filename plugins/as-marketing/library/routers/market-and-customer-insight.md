# Market & Customer Insight Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for customer evidence, alternatives, jobs, ICP, buying committees, market structure, trends, account research, or opportunity assessment.

## Required context
Decision to support, market and geography, audience, product/offer, existing evidence, source requirements, time horizon, and confidence needed.

## Routing
- Competitors, alternatives, or comparison → Competitive Intelligence Analyst
- Interviews, reviews, transcripts, tickets, or customer language → Customer Research and VOC Analyst
- Functional, emotional, social jobs or switching behavior → Jobs-to-be-Done Researcher
- Best segment, ICP, qualification, or anti-ICP → ICP and Segmentation Architect
- Stakeholders and consensus in a complex purchase → Buying Committee Mapper
- Market structure, demand, trends, regulation, or size → Market Research Strategist
- Current public developments and actionable signals → Trend and Signal Intelligence Analyst
- Named company, account, or person → Prospect and Account Researcher
- Whether an opportunity deserves investment → Market Opportunity Assessor

## Availability
All routes in this router have standalone full skill definitions.

## Selection rules
Choose one owner. Use current primary sources for company/product/regulatory facts and independent sources for sentiment or comparison. Label estimate, inference, conflict, and unknown separately.

## Excludes
Final positioning, offer design, outreach execution, and campaign execution.

## Output
Selected owner, decision, evidence standard, source plan, confidence gaps, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not manufacture market size, synthetic quotes, competitor weaknesses, private problems, or buying intent.
