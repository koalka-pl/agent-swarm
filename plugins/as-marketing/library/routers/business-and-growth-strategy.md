# Business & Growth Strategy Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for business-model direction, growth strategy, constraints, channel allocation, compounding loops, founder dependency, offer evolution, market entry, GTM, resource allocation, business cases, or partnerships.

## Required context
Company context, current stage, business-model profile, objective, constraints, customer evidence, offer, economics, acquisition, delivery capacity, founder goals, and time horizon.

## Routing
- How should the business create, deliver, and capture value? → Business Model Strategist
- How should the business grow? → Growth Strategy Architect
- Why is current growth activity not producing results? → Growth Constraint Diagnostician
- Which channels deserve limited time or resources? → Channel Portfolio Prioritizer
- How can acquisition, delivery, retention, or referrals compound? → Growth Loop Designer
- Is founder dependency limiting scale? → Founder Dependency and Scale Auditor
- How should the current offer evolve? → Offer Evolution Planner
- How can bespoke expertise become repeatable? → Service Productization Architect
- How should authority progress into revenue? → Authority-to-Revenue Planner
- How should proof and referrals reinforce growth? → Referral and Proof Loop Designer
- Entering a new market, segment, country, or vertical → Market Entry Strategist
- Launching an offer for a defined audience → Go-to-Market Strategist
- Translate strategy into sequenced quarterly/monthly priorities → Strategic Planning Architect
- Prepare trigger-based decisions under uncertainty → Scenario and Risk Planner
- Several initiatives competing for resources → Resource Allocation Prioritizer
- Financial or executive justification required → Business Case Builder
- Partnership-level strategic decision → Partnership Strategy Designer

## Availability
All routes in this router now have standalone full skill definitions inside the Generic MAS Starter directory.

## Selection rules
Choose one owner; identify the current constraint; route execution downstream. If monetization is unclear, start with Business Model Strategist. If activity is failing for unknown reasons, start with Growth Constraint Diagnostician.

## Excludes
Detailed campaign execution, paid media, copywriting, CRM implementation, analytics implementation, and website building.

## Output
Selected owner, rationale, constraint, required evidence, assumptions, excluded alternatives, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not select an aspirational model without demand evidence or recommend more channels by default. Fail closed if a registered path is missing from the generic runtime.
