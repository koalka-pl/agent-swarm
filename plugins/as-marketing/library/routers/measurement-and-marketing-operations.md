# Measurement & Marketing Operations Router

## Purpose
Select one owner skill for measurement design, analytics, attribution, funnels, visitor intelligence, ROI, forecasting, tracking, governance, automation, integrations, or reporting.

## Required context
Business decision, funnel or lifecycle, metrics, systems, data sources, identities, current instrumentation, owners, cadence, privacy constraints, and implementation access.

## Routing
- Define a business-linked metric system → Marketing Measurement Architect
- Explain changes and drivers in existing data → Marketing Analytics Analyst
- Design a practical influence/attribution model → Attribution Strategist
- Diagnose stage conversion and time-based drop-offs → Funnel Analytics Specialist
- Prioritize identified visitors or companies → Visitor Intelligence Analyst
- Relate spend and effort to qualified outcomes → Marketing ROI Analyst
- Model conditional future outcomes → Forecasting and Scenario Analyst
- Specify events, properties, identities, and QA → Tracking Plan Architect
- Implement or validate GA4/GTM measurement → GA4 and GTM Measurement Specialist
- Govern campaign-link taxonomy → UTM Governance Designer
- Design recurring or trigger-based workflows → Marketing Automation Architect
- Design safe data movement across systems → Integration Workflow Designer
- Specify decision-focused reports and dashboards → Reporting and Dashboard Designer

## Rules
Choose one owner and at most two specialists. Start with the decision, not the available tool. Define terms before dashboards or automation. Name one source of truth per state. Separate implementation, observation, interpretation, and causality.

## Output
Selected owner, rationale, required inputs, data limitations, dependencies, excluded routes, and next routing decision.

## Guardrails
Do not create metrics without owners, mix incompatible denominators, expose PII, invent benchmarks, or claim complete attribution.
