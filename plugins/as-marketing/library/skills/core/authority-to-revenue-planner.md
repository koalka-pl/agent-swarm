# Authority-to-Revenue Planner

## Purpose

Convert expert content and reputation into qualified conversations, paid engagements, implementations, proof, and referrals.

## Owns

The path from expertise assets to commercial conversations for founder-led expert businesses.

## Use when

A consultant, technical specialist, or expert publishes useful content but it is not producing enough qualified pipeline.

## Required inputs

Authority assets, target buyers, offer, existing audience, distribution channels, relationships, content performance, conversion path, and available time.

## Method

1. Inventory articles, demos, projects, frameworks, datasets, talks, and relationships.
2. For each asset define who should care, what problem they recognize, what question follows, and what offer naturally connects.
3. Map:

```text
Expertise → Proof → Relevant Attention → Conversation → Offer → Delivery → Proof
```

4. Add active distribution: identify relevant people, find fresh triggers, prepare question-led comments, share only with context, record interaction, and define follow-up.
5. Design offer transition: exchange perspective → understand process → confirm problem → offer scoped assessment/pilot.
6. Build the pilot-to-proof loop.

## Output

- Authority asset inventory
- Target-buyer map
- Content-to-conversation map
- Distribution plan
- Conversation triggers
- Offer transition
- Pilot-to-proof loop
- Weekly rhythm
- Metrics

## Metrics

Relevant subscribers, qualified replies, relevant profile/site visits, conversations, discovery calls, proposals, paid projects, implementations, references, and introductions.

## Success criteria

Every major authority asset has a defined audience, distribution path, conversation trigger, and commercial next step.

## Guardrails

- Authority is an intermediate asset, not the final outcome.
- Do not optimize only for followers or reactions.
- Do not treat peers as buyers without evidence.
- Do not send generic unsolicited article links.
- Preserve manual review for comments and outreach.
