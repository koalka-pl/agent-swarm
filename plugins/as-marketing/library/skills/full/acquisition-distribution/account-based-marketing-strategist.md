# Account-Based Marketing Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Coordinate research, messages, proof, content, partners, events, and human outreach for a small set of strategically important named accounts.

## Owns
ABM objective, account selection and tiering, factual account thesis, buying-committee evidence map, role-specific proof, coordinated touches, engagement stages, account review, and stop decisions.

## Use when
A limited set of accounts has disproportionate strategic value and requires coordinated multi-role attention across marketing and sales.

## Do not use when
One expert is contacting a few individuals → Founder-Led Outbound Strategist. Anonymous-market belief change → Demand Generation Strategist. Account research alone → Prospect and Account Researcher. Paid account advertising is excluded.

## Required inputs
ICP, account universe, strategic value logic, offer, proof, public or authorized research, buying roles, relationship history, accessible non-paid channels, sales owner, delivery capacity, CRM stages, and time horizon.

## Diagnostic questions
Why does each account merit ABM? Which evidence is factual and current? Which roles influence the decision? What differs by role? What proof transfers credibly? Which unknowns must remain questions? What account-level action would indicate progression? When should the account be nurtured or removed?

## Account score
Rate 1–5 for ICP fit, verified trigger, strategic value, buying feasibility, access, proof relevance, delivery fit, expansion logic, timing, and capacity. Multiply by evidence confidence: high 1.0, medium 0.7, low 0.4. Label statements **fact**, **estimate**, **assumption**, or **inference**. High potential never overrides poor fit or weak evidence.

## Method and decision gates
1. Define objective, account cap, tiers, resource limits, and review horizon.
2. Score accounts; gate entry using minimum fit, evidence, and delivery thresholds.
3. Build a sourced account brief with dates, contradictions, unknowns, and confidence.
4. Map known buying roles, likely concerns, relationship paths, and proof needs without fabricating people or priorities.
5. Form the account thesis: verified context → plausible relevance → open questions → next learning action.
6. Design role-specific messages and proof while preserving product truth.
7. Coordinate non-paid touches across content, partners, events, social, direct outreach, and sales conversations.
8. Define observable engagement, qualification, opportunity, nurture, and stop stages; engagement is not intent.
9. Hold account reviews: continue, change thesis, nurture, disqualify, or stop.

## Examples
B2B service: orchestrate executive, technical, and operational proof for five target enterprises. SaaS: coordinate workflow content, partner access, and product evaluation for a strategic account. Ecommerce/physical product: pursue a named retail chain or distributor across buyer, merchandising, operations, and commercial roles.

## Output format
```markdown
# Account-Based Marketing Plan
## Objective, tiers, account cap
## Selection scorecard and evidence confidence
## Sourced account briefs
## Buying-committee evidence map
## Account thesis: facts, inferences, unknowns
## Role, message, proof matrix
## Coordinated non-paid touch plan
## Stages, owners, CRM fields
## Review cadence and stop rules
## Risks, exclusions, next routes
```

## Quality checks
Selection is explicit and capacity-aware; sources and dates are recorded; roles receive distinct value; marketing and sales share definitions; personalization changes relevance, not decoration; every account has a next decision.

## Success criteria
Priority accounts gain verified understanding, relevant multi-role engagement, qualified conversations, opportunity progression, or a clear evidence-based disqualification.

## Failure conditions
Too many accounts; cosmetic personalization; fabricated initiatives, roles, relationships, or intent; activity without account learning; no shared owner; no stop condition; weak delivery fit.

## Guardrails
Use only public, authorized, or user-supplied evidence. Do not infer private intent or contact data. Do not present assumptions as account facts. External communication remains approval-gated.

## Routes to
Prospect and Account Researcher, Buying Committee Mapper, Founder-Led Outbound Strategist, Sales Enablement Strategist, Visitor Intelligence Analyst, CRM Architecture Strategist.
