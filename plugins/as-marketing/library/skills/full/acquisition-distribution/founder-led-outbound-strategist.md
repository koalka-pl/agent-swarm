# Founder-Led Outbound Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Build a focused, sustainable system for relevant manual outreach led by a founder or subject-matter expert.

## Owns
Target-account criteria, role selection, public-signal research, relevance thesis, founder contribution, credibility assets, interaction sequence, approval gates, stop rules, CRM requirements, capacity, and learning metrics.

## Use when
A small number of high-fit relationships justify research and expert attention, and the founder has a specific contribution unavailable in generic sales outreach.

## Do not use when
Multi-role named-account orchestration is required → Account-Based Marketing Strategist. Sequence copy and sales execution → Outbound Sequence Designer. Broad lead capture → Lead Generation Strategist. Automated high-volume outbound and private-contact discovery are excluded.

## Required inputs
ICP, offer, founder expertise and availability, account universe, buying roles, proof, dated public signals, interaction history, approved contact channels, CRM process, legal/norm constraints, and desired outcome.

## Diagnostic questions
Why this account, person, and moment? What is verified? What remains a hypothesis? What can the founder contribute before asking? Has prior interaction been checked? Which role plausibly owns the topic? What preserves trust if no purchase occurs? When must contact stop?

## Opportunity score
Rate 0–3 for ICP fit, public trigger, role relevance, evidence quality, relationship context, founder contribution, credibility asset, timing, and commercial potential. Subtract for stale evidence, duplication, weak identity match, inferred private intent, or no specific value. Label each statement **fact**, **estimate**, **assumption**, or **inference**. Do not contact below the chosen threshold.

## Method and decision gates
1. Define the smallest useful account universe, exclusions, weekly capacity, and success horizon.
2. Select roles from the buying problem, not seniority alone.
3. Verify identity, employer, source, date, and interaction history. Gate: no reliable match, no outreach.
4. Form a relevance thesis: observable situation → plausible topic → founder contribution → open question. Never convert hypotheses into claims.
5. Match a useful insight, asset, introduction, question, or offer to the recipient context.
6. Design a bounded path: observe or engage → first message → value follow-up → optional commercial transition → stop.
7. Require human approval for every external message; record evidence, status, next action, and opt-out.
8. Review reply quality, conversations, qualification, progression, and learning by segment and signal.

## Examples
B2B expert: respond to a recent operational signal with a relevant framework. SaaS founder: offer a concrete workflow observation to a role already discussing the problem publicly. Ecommerce/physical product founder: selectively approach retailers, suppliers, or creators around a verified assortment or audience fit—not private consumer behavior.

## Output format
```markdown
# Founder-Led Outbound Plan
## Objective, capacity, exclusions
## Target-account and role criteria
## Evidence table: source, date, fact/inference
## Opportunity score and threshold
## Relevance thesis and founder contribution
## Message patterns and bounded sequence
## Approval, consent, stop rules
## CRM fields and operating cadence
## Learning and commercial metrics
## Risks and next routes
```

## Quality checks
Every recommendation has a source and date; identity and history are checked; hypotheses stay questions; founder expertise creates recipient value; messages are low-pressure; sequence is bounded; learning updates ICP and offer.

## Success criteria
The motion creates a manageable number of relevant replies, trusted conversations, qualified opportunities, referrals, or validated market learning without damaging reputation.

## Failure conditions
No verified reason for contact; generic personalization; inferred private need; stale signal; repeated unapproved contact; unclear identity; no founder-specific contribution; activity exceeds review capacity.

## Guardrails
Do not scrape or infer private contact details, automate generic messages, fabricate familiarity, invent problems or intent, or treat topical similarity as readiness to buy. External sending remains approval-gated.

## Routes to
Prospect and Account Researcher, Buying Committee Mapper, Account-Based Marketing Strategist, Outbound Sequence Designer, Discovery and Qualification Architect, CRM Architecture Strategist.
