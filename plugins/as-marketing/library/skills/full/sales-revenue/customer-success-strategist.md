# Customer Success Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Help customers reach meaningful value, adopt the solution, surface risk, create permissioned proof, and identify appropriate continuation.

## Owns
Customer success definition, onboarding and value journey, milestones, shared responsibilities, health dimensions, risk playbooks, review cadence, value evidence, proof permission, and handoffs.

## Use when
A business needs a repeatable system for onboarding, adoption, outcome realization, risk management, and customer value reviews.

## Do not use when
Pilot production decision → Pilot-to-Implementation Planner. Commercial renewal/expansion → Expansion and Renewal Planner. Support operations alone are not customer success strategy.

## Required inputs
Customer goals, offer and commitments, implementation plan, users and stakeholders, time-to-value, product/service signals, support and delivery history, contract/renewal model, economics, risks, and ownership.

## Diagnostic questions
What outcome does the customer define as success? Which milestones demonstrate progress? Who owns each dependency? What is usage versus value? Which risks are leading, lagging, or unknown? How are stakeholder changes handled? What proof can be captured with permission? When is continuation appropriate?

## Customer health score
Rate outcome, adoption, implementation, relationship, stakeholder support, delivery, support burden, risk, and commercial status separately on 1–5 with evidence confidence. Label inputs **fact**, **estimate**, **assumption**, or **inference**. Never hide a critical risk in one average score.

## Method and decision gates
1. Translate sold commitments into customer-defined outcomes, scope, responsibilities, and evidence.
2. Map onboarding, setup, activation, first value, adoption, outcome, review, renewal, and advocacy.
3. Define milestones, owners, dependencies, expected timing, evidence, and escalation.
4. Build multidimensional health signals with source, freshness, threshold, owner, and response.
5. Create playbooks for onboarding delay, low adoption, blocked implementation, poor outcome, support escalation, stakeholder change, and delivery failure.
6. Establish customer review cadence focused on outcomes, gaps, decisions, and next actions.
7. Capture reusable proof only with explicit permission, scope, and accuracy review.
8. Route renewal or expansion only after delivered value and remaining jobs are evidenced.

## Examples
B2B service: shared implementation plan, milestone reviews, and outcome evidence. SaaS: onboarding, activation, role adoption, integration, support, and business-value review. Ecommerce/physical product B2B: retailer/distributor onboarding, assortment launch, fulfillment quality, sell-through or usage evidence, service issues, and reorder readiness.

## Output format
```markdown
# Customer Success Strategy
## Customer outcomes and sold commitments
## Journey and time-to-value
## Milestones, owners, dependencies
## Health dimensions and evidence
## Risk and escalation playbooks
## Customer review cadence
## Value evidence and proof permission
## Renewal/expansion handoffs
## Metrics, failure thresholds, next routes
```

## Quality checks
Success is defined in customer terms; usage is not equated with value; milestones have owners; risk signals trigger action; delivery failures stay visible; proof requires permission; commercial handoffs follow value.

## Success criteria
Customers reach agreed value with transparent progress, risks are addressed early, and continuation decisions rely on evidence rather than pressure or usage alone.

## Failure conditions
Health average hides critical risk; onboarding is task-only; promises do not reach delivery; support volume treated as engagement; testimony collected without permission; expansion precedes outcome.

## Guardrails
Do not hide delivery failure, manipulate health, invent value, or pressure advocacy, renewal, or expansion. Respect customer confidentiality and permission boundaries.

## Routes to
Pilot-to-Implementation Planner, Expansion and Renewal Planner, Referral Program Designer, Revenue Operations Architect.
