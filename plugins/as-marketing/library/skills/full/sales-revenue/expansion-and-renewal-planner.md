# Expansion and Renewal Planner

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Plan appropriate renewal or follow-on value using delivered outcomes, remaining jobs, relationship health, implementation capacity, and timing.

## Owns
Renewal readiness, delivered-value review, risk visibility, remaining-job evidence, expansion thesis, stakeholder path, commercial timing, decision milestones, stop conditions, and handoff to proposal/offer owners.

## Use when
A customer relationship approaches renewal or has credible adjacent value opportunities after meaningful delivery evidence exists.

## Do not use when
Onboarding and value realization → Customer Success Strategist. New offer design → Offer Architecture Strategist. Proposal writing → Proposal and Business Case Writer. Calendar pressure alone is not expansion evidence.

## Required inputs
Original goals and commitments, delivered outcomes, adoption/use, health, unresolved issues, stakeholder map, remaining jobs, adjacent teams/use cases, contract terms, economics, capacity, delivery risk, and decision dates.

## Diagnostic questions
What was promised and delivered? Which gaps remain? Is renewal continuity distinct from expansion? What adjacent need is evidenced? Who benefits, owns, approves, and implements? Does timing reflect customer value or seller calendar? Can operations support added scope? What would make “not now” or “no” correct?

## Opportunity readiness score
Rate 1–5 for delivered value, problem evidence, stakeholder support, timing, solution fit, operational readiness, economic value, proof, relationship health, and risk. Label inputs **fact**, **customer-stated**, **estimate**, **assumption**, or **inference**. Unresolved critical success issues block expansion pressure.

## Method and decision gates
1. Compare original goals, promises, outcomes, adoption, and unresolved gaps.
2. Assess health, trust, stakeholder support, delivery quality, and risk separately.
3. Separate renewal decision from new expansion scope and evidence.
4. Identify remaining or adjacent jobs through customer evidence, not seller convenience.
5. Evaluate fit, value, effort, dependencies, timing, economics, capacity, and buying path.
6. Build an expansion thesis: evidenced job → expected value → proof → scope hypothesis → open questions.
7. Map stakeholders, internal case, commercial path, decision dates, success criteria, and no-pressure next action.
8. Define continue, renew-only, validate, defer, redesign, or stop with explicit reasons.

## Examples
B2B service: renew core support separately from a new implementation phase. SaaS: expand seats, teams, modules, or use cases only after adoption and outcome evidence. Ecommerce/physical product B2B: renew distribution terms and assess new assortment, geography, volume, or channel using sell-through, margin, fulfillment, and support evidence.

## Output format
```markdown
# Renewal and Expansion Plan
## Original commitments and delivered value
## Health, gaps, risks
## Renewal readiness
## Evidence-backed adjacent jobs
## Expansion score and thesis
## Stakeholder and buying path
## Scope/value assumptions and capacity
## Decision timeline and next actions
## Stop/defer conditions and next routes
```

## Quality checks
Renewal and expansion are distinct; delivered value is evidenced; unresolved risk is visible; adjacent demand is not invented; operations can support scope; customer timing governs progression.

## Success criteria
Renewal and expansion decisions reflect real customer value, transparent risks, appropriate scope, and mutual readiness, preserving trust even when the answer is defer or stop.

## Failure conditions
Calendar-driven pressure; hidden success issues; usage called outcome; adjacent job assumed; renewal held hostage to expansion; capacity ignored; proposal precedes evidence.

## Guardrails
Do not invent demand, value, stakeholder support, or urgency. Do not pressure customers before outcomes or conceal delivery failures and contract constraints.

## Routes to
Customer Success Strategist, Offer Architecture Strategist, Buying Committee Mapper, Proposal and Business Case Writer.
