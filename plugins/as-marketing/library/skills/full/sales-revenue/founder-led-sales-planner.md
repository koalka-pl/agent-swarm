# Founder-Led Sales Planner

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Build a sustainable sales process where founder expertise improves trust and learning without making every deal permanently founder-dependent.

## Owns
Founder role by stage, sales-process architecture, high-leverage interactions, preparation/follow-up routines, capacity rules, delegation path, learning loop, and transition criteria.

## Use when
The founder materially improves diagnosis, credibility, product insight, or commercial confidence and the business needs repeatability without premature removal.

## Do not use when
Initial prospecting → Founder-Led Outbound Strategist. Discovery framework → Discovery and Qualification Architect. Pipeline governance → Pipeline Process Designer. General founder dependency audit → Founder Dependency and Scale Auditor.

## Required inputs
Offer, ICP, sales cycle, founder expertise and capacity, active conversations, proof, qualification, demo/proposal process, CRM, delivery handoff, targets, team capability, and historical outcomes.

## Diagnostic questions
Where does founder presence change decision quality? Which moments require expertise versus status? What can be standardized safely? What can be delegated now, shadowed, or retained? How much founder capacity exists? Which learning must return to product, offer, and messaging?

## Process readiness score
Rate 1–5 for qualification clarity, founder leverage, buyer-progress evidence, repeatability, follow-up reliability, proof use, capacity fit, record quality, handoff, and delegation readiness. Label inputs **fact**, **estimate**, **assumption**, or **inference**. Do not remove founder involvement solely for scale when it remains strategically necessary.

## Method and decision gates
1. Define sales objective, cycle, deal types, founder capacity, and bottleneck.
2. Map stages from first qualified conversation through discovery, solution fit, proposal, decision, and delivery handoff.
3. Define buyer-progress evidence, owner, founder role, preparation, output, and exit for each stage.
4. Reserve founder time for diagnosis, credibility, complex objection, vision, or executive alignment where evidence supports leverage.
5. Standardize preparation, discovery, demonstration, proof, follow-up, and commercial transitions without scripting away judgment.
6. Set weekly capacity, prioritization, pipeline review, CRM hygiene, and no-show/stale rules.
7. Assign retain, document, shadow, delegate, automate-later, or stop to recurring activities.
8. Build learning loops from calls and outcomes into ICP, offer, proof, product, and enablement.

## Examples
B2B expert service: founder leads diagnosis and executive alignment; team owns scheduling, records, follow-up, and delivery preparation. SaaS: founder joins high-value workflow or technical calls while repeatable demos move to a specialist. Physical product/ecommerce B2B: founder handles strategic retail or manufacturing conversations while standard qualification and fulfillment handoff are delegated.

## Output format
```markdown
# Founder-Led Sales System
## Objective, constraints, founder capacity
## Sales stages and buyer evidence
## Founder leverage map
## Call, preparation, follow-up routines
## Proof and enablement needs
## CRM and weekly review cadence
## Retain / shadow / delegate roadmap
## Learning loop and metrics
## Risks, stop rules, next routes
```

## Quality checks
Founder time is used at high-leverage moments; qualification protects capacity; stages reflect buyer progress; records survive delegation; handoff protects promises; learning updates strategy.

## Success criteria
The process improves decision quality and revenue learning while increasing reliability, preserving trust, and reducing avoidable founder bottlenecks.

## Failure conditions
Founder attends every call by default; intuition replaces records; delegation occurs before process clarity; scripts suppress expertise; follow-up is inconsistent; sales promises do not reach delivery.

## Guardrails
Do not manufacture pipeline certainty or remove founder involvement where it creates material value. External commitments require approval and alignment with delivery capacity.

## Routes to
Discovery and Qualification Architect, Pipeline Process Designer, CRM Architecture Strategist, Sales Enablement Strategist, Founder Dependency and Scale Auditor.
