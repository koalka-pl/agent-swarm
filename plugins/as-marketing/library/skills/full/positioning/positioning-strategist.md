# Positioning Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Define the best-fit audience, buying situation, competitive context, differentiated value, proof, and reason to choose.

## Owns
Positioning decision, market frame, alternatives, valuable differentiation, mechanism, proof requirements, exclusions, implications, and validation.

## Use when
The business needs to decide what an offer/company should be known for and why a specific audience should choose it.

## Do not use when
Customer evidence is weak → Customer Research and VOC Analyst. Approved positioning needs communication hierarchy → Messaging Architect. Final copy → Conversion Copywriter.

## Required inputs
ICP, trigger, customer research, alternatives, capabilities, offer, outcomes, proof, competitive evidence, buying process, economics, and constraints.

## Diagnostic questions
1. Which customers receive disproportionate value?
2. What situation triggers change?
3. What would they use without this solution?
4. Which capability is meaningfully different and defensible?
5. How does capability create valuable outcome?
6. What proof supports the chain?
7. Which category frame reduces buyer confusion?
8. Who or what should be excluded?

## Positioning score
Rate 1–5 for ICP relevance, urgency, differentiation, credibility, category comprehension, proof coverage, commercial fit, and durability; rate confidence separately 0–3. Any option below 3 on relevance or credibility fails.

## Method and decision gates
1. Select best-fit customer and buying situation.
2. Map direct, internal, manual, status quo, and non-consumption alternatives.
3. Inventory capabilities and remove differences buyers do not value.
4. Connect capability → mechanism → outcome → proof.
5. Compare existing-category, subcategory, and new-frame options.
6. Draft alternatives and score them.
7. Define implications for messaging, offer, proof, content, sales, product, and delivery.
8. Design evidence tests before broad rollout.

## Examples
- **B2B service:** specialist operating method versus internal coordination and generalist consulting.
- **SaaS:** fastest reliable path to a repeated workflow outcome, not “all-in-one platform.”
- **Physical product/ecommerce:** distinct use occasion and verified product mechanism versus price-only differentiation.

## Output format
```markdown
# Positioning Decision
## Best-fit audience and trigger
## Alternatives
## Differentiated capability
## Mechanism-to-outcome chain
## Category frame
## Positioning statement
## Proof requirements
## Exclusions and risks
## Cross-functional implications
## Validation plan
```

## Quality checks
Names a real alternative; differentiation is valuable and evidenced; category reduces confusion; outcome is customer-level; exclusions protect focus; positioning changes operations.

## Success criteria
The decision gives messaging, offer, sales, content, product, and proof teams one coherent strategic frame that can be tested in the market.

## Failure conditions
Broad audience; feature difference without value; invented differentiation; unsupported superlative; category education burden ignored; no implications or test.

## Guardrails
Do not invent differentiation, force novelty, claim uncontested category ownership, or hide weak evidence behind wording.

## Routes to
Messaging Architect, Value Proposition Designer, Category Design Strategist, Product Marketing Strategist, Proof and Credibility Architect.
