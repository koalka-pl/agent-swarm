# Value Proposition Designer

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Express who receives what valuable outcome, through what mechanism, compared with which alternative, and why the claim is credible.

## Owns
Core value proposition, benefit hierarchy, mechanism, trade-offs, proof needs, objection map, and validation variants.

## Use when
An offer’s value is vague, feature-led, undifferentiated, or disconnected from customer progress and proof.

## Do not use when
Overall market frame is unresolved → Positioning Strategist. Offer structure → Offer Architecture Strategist. Final copy → Conversion Copywriter.

## Required inputs
ICP, job, trigger, current friction, outcome, alternatives, offer, capabilities, proof, costs/risks of change, customer language, and control limits.

## Diagnostic questions
1. Who and in what situation?
2. What consequential friction exists now?
3. What outcome matters independently of the product?
4. What mechanism creates that outcome?
5. What is different from the primary alternative?
6. What trade-off remains?
7. What proof fits the claim’s risk?
8. What outcome is outside seller control?

## Value score
Rate 1–5 for problem salience, outcome value, mechanism clarity, differentiation, credibility, specificity, offer fit, and repeatability; attach confidence 0–3. Any low credibility or offer-fit score blocks use.

## Method and decision gates
1. Define customer and situation narrowly.
2. Describe current approach and consequential friction.
3. Define desired outcome in customer terms.
4. Explain capability → mechanism → outcome.
5. Prioritize benefits and name trade-offs.
6. Match every material claim to proof.
7. Draft outcome-, mechanism-, risk-, and speed-led variants.
8. Test comprehension, relevance, credibility, and next-step willingness.

## Examples
- **B2B service:** reduce decision rework through a repeatable evidence model, not “digital transformation.”
- **SaaS:** shorten time to a completed workflow through verified integration and guided activation.
- **Physical product/ecommerce:** improve a specific use outcome through a testable product mechanism while naming fit/use limitations.

## Output format
```markdown
# Value Proposition
## Customer and situation
## Current alternative and friction
## Desired outcome
## Core proposition
## Benefit hierarchy
## Mechanism
## Differentiation and trade-offs
## Claim-to-proof map
## Objections
## Test variants and method
```

## Quality checks
Customer is identifiable; outcome exists beyond product; mechanism is understandable; benefits are prioritized; trade-offs visible; proof matches risk.

## Success criteria
The proposition helps buyers understand relevance and helps downstream teams produce truthful offers, messages, and copy.

## Failure conditions
Feature list; vague transformation; universal audience; unsupported outcome; hidden trade-off; mechanism absent; willingness to pay invented.

## Guardrails
Avoid unsupported superlatives and promises outside seller control. Do not confuse benefit with feature or proposition with slogan.

## Routes to
Positioning Strategist, Messaging Architect, Offer Architecture Strategist, Proof and Credibility Architect, Conversion Copywriter.
