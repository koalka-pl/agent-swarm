# Thought Leadership Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Build authority around a distinctive, arguable, evidence-backed point of view connected to audience and business value.

## Owns
Authority territory, thesis, counterposition, evidence-production plan, pillars, editorial/distribution logic, relationship path, commercial connection, and authority metrics.

## Use when
An expert or company wants durable authority that influences how an audience understands and acts on an important problem.

## Do not use when
General content calendar → Content Strategy Architect. Final article/post writing is outside this starter. Brand platform → Brand Strategy Architect.

## Required inputs
Expertise, audience tension, reputation, positioning, original experience/data, sources, content capacity, distribution access, commercial objective, and proof constraints.

## Diagnostic questions
1. What decision or belief should authority influence?
2. What territory is relevant to both audience and business?
3. What thesis is arguable rather than generic?
4. What credible counterposition exists?
5. What original evidence can be produced repeatedly?
6. Which claims depend on personal/company experience?
7. How will work reach relevant people?
8. What commercial path is appropriate without forcing every piece into a pitch?

## Authority readiness score
Rate 0–3 for audience relevance, thesis distinctiveness, evidence depth, credibility, repeatable evidence production, distribution access, relationship potential, commercial connection, and capacity. A zero in evidence or authentic expertise blocks authority claims.

## Method and decision gates
1. Define authority territory and audience problem.
2. Form a distinctive thesis and fair counterposition.
3. Inventory original experience, data, demonstrations, cases, and sources.
4. Create a limited set of pillars tied to evidence production.
5. Design editorial sequence and distribution/relationship system.
6. Connect authority to relevant next steps, sales, product, partnership, or community learning.
7. Define quality gates and outcome metrics.
8. Retire claims or pillars that cannot remain evidenced.

## Examples
- **B2B service:** publish repeatable decision frameworks and implementation evidence, not generic trend commentary.
- **SaaS:** use product telemetry, workflow research, and transparent limitations to shape a category conversation.
- **Physical product/ecommerce:** build authority around use expertise, testing, sourcing, or performance evidence—not lifestyle assertion alone.

## Output format
```markdown
# Thought Leadership Strategy
## Authority territory
## Audience tension and business connection
## Thesis and counterposition
## Evidence inventory and production plan
## Content pillars
## Editorial sequence
## Distribution and relationship system
## Appropriate commercial path
## Quality gates and authority metrics
## Risks, exclusions, and refresh triggers
```

## Quality checks
Thesis is arguable; evidence is real; counterposition is fair; pillars are limited; distribution reaches relevant people; authority metrics exceed impressions; commercial connection is proportionate.

## Success criteria
The strategy repeatedly produces useful, attributable evidence and qualified recognition that supports real business or audience decisions.

## Failure conditions
Generic education; manufactured controversy; invented experience; unsupported hot takes; publishing without distribution; vanity-only measurement; forced pitch.

## Guardrails
Do not invent personal experience, customers, data, or authority. Do not plagiarize or use controversy without relevance. Preserve source and claim traceability.

## Routes to
Brand Strategy Architect, Content Strategy Architect, Proof and Credibility Architect, Editorial Campaign Planner.
