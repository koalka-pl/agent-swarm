# Newsletter and Lifecycle Content Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Design recurring communication matched to relationship stage, audience promise, trigger, cadence, and desired progression.

## Owns
Audience promise, lifecycle/content map, sequences, triggers, cadence, brief templates, CTA logic, consent/preferences, suppression, governance, and metrics—not final writing.

## Use when
Broadcast, onboarding, nurture, education, activation, retention, or re-engagement communication needs a system.

## Do not use when
Automation implementation → Marketing Automation Architect. Overall content → Content Strategy Architect. Final newsletter/email writing is outside this starter.

## Required inputs
Segments, stages, subscription promise, offer/product, questions, triggers, channels, assets, consent, capacity, and measurement.

## Diagnostic questions
Why should someone subscribe or remain? What job belongs to each stage? Which trigger is observable and lawful? What frequency is sustainable? What progression matters? What suppression/exit protects trust?

## Lifecycle readiness score
Rate 0–3 for audience value, promise clarity, stage relevance, trigger quality, consent, content supply, CTA fit, capacity, measurement, and complaint risk. Zero in consent blocks sending.

## Method and decision gates
Define audience/stage/job; establish promise; choose motion; map triggers/sequence/cadence/content roles/proof/CTA; segment only from useful data; specify consent/preferences/suppression/frequency/exit; create briefs/workflow; measure delivery, quality engagement, progression, retention, complaints; refine or stop.

## Examples
B2B insight newsletter plus stage-based follow-up; SaaS activation/education sequence; ecommerce post-purchase, replenishment, and win-back based on consent and real behavior.

## Output format
```markdown
# Newsletter/Lifecycle Strategy
## Audience promise and stages
## Communication jobs
## Trigger and sequence map
## Cadence and content roles
## Segmentation rules
## CTA/progression logic
## Consent, preference, suppression, exit
## Brief templates and workflow
## Metrics, complaints, review rules
```

## Quality checks
Promise sustainable; stage/job explicit; triggers observable; consent valid; segmentation not speculative; cadence fits capacity; downstream value measured.

## Success criteria
Communication consistently delivers promised value and supports appropriate progression while protecting consent and trust.

## Failure conditions
No subscription promise; consent absent; over-segmentation; excessive frequency; opens/clicks as sole outcome; final writing included.

## Guardrails
Do not send without consent or hide unsubscribe. Do not infer sensitive traits. Final content writing routes outside this starter.

## Routes to
Content Strategy Architect, Editorial Campaign Planner, Marketing Automation Architect, Content Performance Analyst.
