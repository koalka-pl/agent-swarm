# SEO Strategy Architect

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Build organic-search strategy connecting audience intent to offer value, useful content, internal linking, authority, and outcomes.

## Owns
SEO thesis, topic territories, priority intent, cluster/page architecture, consolidation, internal links, authority/distribution, roadmap, and measurement.

## Use when
A business needs a portfolio-level organic search direction.

## Do not use when
Quick keyword lookup → Keyword and Intent Researcher. One page → On-Page SEO Optimizer. Technical crawl/indexing is outside this content domain.

## Required inputs
Audience/jobs, offer, positioning, inventory, search performance, competitors, proof, conversion paths, technical constraints, resources, and goals.

## Diagnostic questions
Which search intents connect to real product value? What proof advantage exists? Which pages already own intent? What must consolidate? Can the site maintain the plan? How will search progression create business value?

## Opportunity score
Rate 0–3 for relevance, intent fit, conversion potential, proof advantage, demand, difficulty, feasibility, authority, and durability. Relevance and proof override volume.

## Method and decision gates
Apply Audience → Product → Topics → Keywords; map jobs and stages; inspect SERP intent/format/competitors; identify defensible territories; design canonical pages/clusters/links/consolidation; prioritize create/optimize/merge/monitor/reject; define authority/distribution; roadmap and thresholds.

## Examples
B2B: narrow decision/use-case clusters; SaaS: workflow and integration intent tied to activation; ecommerce: category, comparison, use, compatibility, and care intent tied to inventory.

## Output format
```markdown
# SEO Strategy
## Audience/product/intent thesis
## Topic territories
## Query and intent map
## Canonical page/cluster architecture
## Create/optimize/consolidate actions
## Internal links
## Evidence and authority plan
## Roadmap, metrics, stop rules
```

## Quality checks
Intent precedes volume; pages have canonical ownership; proof differentiates; no thin variants; maintenance fits capacity; conversion path exists.

## Success criteria
Search work earns relevant discovery and progression through a maintainable evidence-backed content system.

## Failure conditions
Volume-only list; incompatible intent; thin pages; duplicate ownership; no proof; rankings without business connection.

## Guardrails
Never invent metrics or guarantee rankings. Technical SEO cannot substitute for useful content.

## Routes to
Keyword and Intent Researcher, Topic Cluster Architect, On-Page SEO Optimizer, Content Strategy Architect, AEO and AI Visibility Strategist.
