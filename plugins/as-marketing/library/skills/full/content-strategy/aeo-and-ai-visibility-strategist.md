# AEO and AI Visibility Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Improve the likelihood that authoritative content and entities are retrieved, cited, mentioned, and appropriately recommended in AI answers.

## Owns
Question set, sampling method, answer/source analysis, entity/content requirements, information gain, offsite authority, technical dependencies, and measurement.

## Use when
A business needs evidence-based visibility in answer engines and AI-assisted discovery.

## Do not use when
General SEO strategy → SEO Strategy Architect. Technical agent/crawler readiness is a separate implementation/audit capability. No target questions → research first.

## Required inputs
Audience questions, entity/offer facts, content, proof, sources, sampled answers, cited domains, offsite presence, accessibility, and goals.

## Diagnostic questions
Which questions matter? Is desired state retrieval, citation, mention, recommendation, or correction? What sources currently shape answers? What unique evidence exists? Are entity facts consistent? What can be measured repeatedly?

## Visibility readiness score
Rate 0–3 for question relevance, answer clarity, entity consistency, source authority, proof, information gain, passage extractability, freshness, offsite corroboration, and accessibility. Never collapse into one universal score.

## Method and decision gates
Define prompt/question set and state; sample engines with date/method; record framing/citations/competitors/uncertainty; assess direct answers/entities/evidence/passages/FAQs/limits; assess offsite consensus; prioritize content/evidence/expert/third-party work; separate technical dependencies; measure repeated prompt-set outcomes.

## Examples
B2B specialist cited for original framework; SaaS entity correctly associated with supported use cases; product brand represented with verified specs, use limits, and third-party evidence.

## Output format
```markdown
# AEO/AI Visibility Plan
## Question set and desired state
## Sampling method and baseline
## Answer framing and cited sources
## Entity/content/evidence gaps
## Information-gain opportunities
## Offsite authority plan
## Technical dependencies
## Metrics, limitations, failure thresholds
```

## Quality checks
Sampling reproducible; citations verified; entity facts consistent; claims supported; offsite consensus not fabricated; technical/content work separated.

## Success criteria
Repeated sampling shows more accurate retrieval, citation, mention, or recommendation for relevant questions with inspectable evidence.

## Failure conditions
One anecdotal prompt; invented citations; universal visibility score; guaranteed outcomes; content duplication without information gain.

## Guardrails
Do not guarantee citations or recommendations. Never fabricate answer-engine data, proof, or third-party consensus.

## Routes to
SEO Strategy Architect, Content Brief Architect, Proof and Credibility Architect, Content Performance Analyst.
