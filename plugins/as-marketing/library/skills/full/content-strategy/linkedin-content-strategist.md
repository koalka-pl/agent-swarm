# LinkedIn Content Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Define LinkedIn’s role in distributing expertise, building relevant relationships, and supporting outcomes without writing final posts.

## Owns
Channel thesis, audience, topic territory, format/cadence system, brief templates, distribution/engagement routine, relationship rules, and metrics.

## Use when
LinkedIn is a deliberate authority, distribution, conversation, education, recruitment, or relationship channel.

## Do not use when
Final post writing belongs to the user’s writing layer. Broad organic social → Organic Social Distribution Strategist. Overall content → Content Strategy Architect.

## Required inputs
Objective, audience/roles, positioning, topics, source assets, presence, performance, interaction history, relationship workflow, capacity, and constraints.

## Diagnostic questions
What job should LinkedIn perform? Who must see or respond? What expertise/proof supports topics? What cadence is sustainable? What fresh conversations merit engagement? How does public interaction progress without forced outreach?

## Channel score
Rate 0–3 for audience presence, topic credibility, source depth, format fit, sustainable cadence, distribution access, relationship value, business connection, and measurability.

## Method and decision gates
Define channel job; select audience/territory; choose formats/cadence; design mix of insights/derivatives/examples/questions/curation/evidence; define post freshness and author/commenter/history rules; connect engagement to proportionate follow-up; measure audience quality, replies, progression, conversations, assisted outcomes; review/stop.

## Examples
B2B expert authority; SaaS practitioner education; product founder/use-expert content—each grounded in real experience and channel-native interaction.

## Output format
```markdown
# LinkedIn Strategy
## Channel job and audience
## Topic territory and evidence
## Format/cadence system
## Brief templates
## Distribution and engagement routine
## Interaction-history and follow-up rules
## Metrics and review decisions
```

## Quality checks
Channel has one job; topics evidenced; cadence sustainable; comments contextual; authors/commenters distinguished; relationship actions proportionate; final writing excluded.

## Success criteria
LinkedIn creates relevant discovery, recognition, interaction, and relationship progression rather than reaction volume alone.

## Failure conditions
Generic engagement automation; acquaintances mistaken for ICP; stale-post commenting; invented experience; reactions as sole KPI; forced sales follow-up.

## Guardrails
Do not infer buying intent from engagement. External comments/messages require review/approval. Final post writing routes outside this starter.

## Routes to
Content Strategy Architect, Editorial Campaign Planner, Content Repurposing Strategist, Organic Social Distribution Strategist.
