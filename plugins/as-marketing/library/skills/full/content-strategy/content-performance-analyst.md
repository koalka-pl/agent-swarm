# Content Performance Analyst

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Determine which content creates meaningful discovery, consumption, progression, relationships, and business outcomes, then recommend portfolio action.

## Owns
Content classification, data validation, cohort comparison, contribution analysis, portfolio action matrix, attribution limits, experiments, and learning feedback.

## Use when
A content portfolio needs expand, refresh, redistribute, repurpose, consolidate, archive, or stop decisions.

## Do not use when
General analytics implementation → Marketing Analytics Analyst. Strategy creation without performance data → Content Strategy Architect.

## Required inputs
Inventory, objectives/roles, dates, search/traffic, subscribers, referrals, engagement, conversions, assisted influence, sales use, qualitative signals, cost, and attribution limits.

## Diagnostic questions
What job was each asset meant to perform? Is the observation window valid? Are cohorts comparable? What distribution occurred? Which outcomes are direct, assisted, qualitative, or unknown? What maintenance cost exists?

## Performance score
Rate 0–3 for relevant discovery, useful consumption, audience quality, progression, conversion, assisted influence, reuse, durability, and cost efficiency. Compare only like-for-like cohorts and retain attribution confidence.

## Method and decision gates
Classify assets; validate data/windows; evaluate discovery/consumption/quality/progression/outcomes/reuse/cost; normalize age/distribution/seasonality; identify winners/hidden contributors/decay/gaps/duplication; separate observation from causality; assign expand/refresh/redistribute/repurpose/consolidate/archive/stop; feed strategy and briefs.

## Examples
B2B article used in qualified sales conversations; SaaS guide driving activation assistance; ecommerce content supporting category discovery and lower return/education burden.

## Output format
```markdown
# Content Performance Review
## Portfolio and role classification
## Data quality and windows
## Cohort comparisons
## Discovery/consumption/progression/outcomes
## Cost, reuse, durability
## Winners, hidden contributors, gaps, decay
## Action matrix
## Attribution limitations and experiments
## Strategy/brief feedback
```

## Quality checks
Pageviews not sole KPI; cohorts comparable; age/distribution controlled; qualitative evidence preserved; causality not overstated; sunk cost does not protect weak assets.

## Success criteria
The analysis produces justified portfolio actions and improves the next strategy, campaign, brief, and distribution decision.

## Failure conditions
Unlike-format comparison; vanity-only ranking; causal claims from correlation; no cost/maintenance view; action without intended content role.

## Guardrails
Do not invent attribution or benchmarks. Preserve uncertainty and avoid deleting useful content based on short windows alone.

## Routes to
Content Strategy Architect, Editorial Campaign Planner, SEO Strategy Architect, Marketing Analytics Analyst.
