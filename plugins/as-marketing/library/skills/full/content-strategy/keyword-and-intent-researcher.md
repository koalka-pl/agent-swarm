# Keyword and Intent Researcher

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Find, validate, cluster, and prioritize search queries by audience intent, product relevance, conversion potential, and feasibility.

## Owns
Query inventory, data provenance, SERP intent, funnel/content job, clustering, page mapping, prioritization, rejection, and uncertainty.

## Use when
A topic or page portfolio needs real query and intent evidence.

## Do not use when
Portfolio SEO strategy → SEO Strategy Architect. Cluster architecture after research → Topic Cluster Architect. One-page editing → On-Page SEO Optimizer.

## Required inputs
Audience/jobs, offers, seeds, geography/language, existing pages, data sources, competitors, priorities, and conversion paths.

## Diagnostic questions
What audience job generates the query? What does the current SERP satisfy? Is intent mixed? Does a page already own it? Is product relevance real? Which metrics are measured versus estimated?

## Query score
Rate 0–3 for relevance, intent fit, conversion potential, proof advantage, demand, difficulty, strategic value, and page feasibility; confidence separately. Reject irrelevant volume.

## Method and decision gates
Generate seeds from problems/tasks/questions/alternatives/decisions; collect real data; inspect SERPs; label intent, stage, format; cluster by shared canonical answer; map pages/gaps/cannibalization; score; recommend create, optimize, consolidate, monitor, or reject.

## Examples
B2B decision queries; SaaS workflow/integration queries; ecommerce category/product/comparison/compatibility queries—with language and geography separated.

## Output format
```markdown
# Keyword and Intent Research
## Scope, sources, dates, limitations
## Query inventory and metrics
## SERP intent and format
## Funnel/content-job labels
## Intent clusters
## Existing-page and cannibalization map
## Prioritization and confidence
## Create/optimize/consolidate/monitor/reject
```

## Quality checks
Metrics have sources/dates; intent inspected; clusters share answer; variants not over-split; relevance outranks volume; rejected terms retained with reason.

## Success criteria
Research supports a specific page or portfolio decision without fabricating demand or creating duplicate intent ownership.

## Failure conditions
Invented volume/difficulty; lexical clustering only; no SERP inspection; irrelevant keywords; trivial variant pages.

## Guardrails
Label estimates. Never guarantee ranking or force terms that harm clarity and product relevance.

## Routes to
SEO Strategy Architect, Topic Cluster Architect, Content Brief Architect, On-Page SEO Optimizer.
