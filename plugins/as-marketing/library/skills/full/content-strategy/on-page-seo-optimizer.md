# On-Page SEO Optimizer

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Improve an existing page for matched search intent while preserving accuracy, editorial quality, and conversion purpose.

## Owns
Intent diagnosis, query mapping, content/entity gaps, exact recommendations, metadata, links, evidence needs, consolidation, and measurement.

## Use when
A specific existing page has target queries or organic-performance opportunity.

## Do not use when
Portfolio SEO → SEO Strategy Architect. Intent research absent → Keyword and Intent Researcher. Technical crawl/indexing is outside this skill.

## Required inputs
Page text/URL, audience, page role, queries, search performance, SERP evidence, links, metadata, freshness, and conversion goal.

## Diagnostic questions
What intent should this page own? Does current SERP support it? What answer/evidence/entity is missing? Is another page the better owner? Which change serves users and conversion? What observation window is valid?

## Optimization score
Rate 0–3 for intent match, answer completeness, evidence, differentiation, entity clarity, freshness, hierarchy, internal links, snippet/CTR fit, and conversion continuity.

## Method and decision gates
Confirm canonical intent; compare SERP format; identify answer/evidence/example/entity/freshness gaps; review title/description/H1/hierarchy/URL/links/images/structured sections; provide exact current → revised placement; avoid stuffing; decide optimize/consolidate/rewrite/reject; define measurement window.

## Examples
B2B service page aligned to decision intent; SaaS integration page aligned to implementation intent; ecommerce category/product page aligned to comparison or purchase intent.

## Output format
```markdown
# On-Page SEO Plan
## Intent and canonical query cluster
## SERP/content comparison
## Gaps and evidence needs
## Exact before/after placements
## Metadata and hierarchy
## Internal links/consolidation
## Conversion continuity
## Metrics, guardrails, review window
```

## Quality checks
Intent compatible; facts unchanged unless sourced; placements exact; keywords natural; canonical ownership clear; conversion purpose preserved.

## Success criteria
The page better satisfies the intended searcher and produces measurable search or downstream improvement without factual drift.

## Failure conditions
Incompatible intent; keyword stuffing; invented data; duplicated page; edits without evidence; ranking guarantee.

## Guardrails
Never invent rankings, volume, or claims. Do not optimize away clarity or conversion purpose.

## Routes to
Keyword and Intent Researcher, Topic Cluster Architect, Content Brief Architect, AEO and AI Visibility Strategist.
