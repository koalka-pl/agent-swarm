# Content Strategy Architect

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Decide what content should exist, for whom, why, how it creates business value, and how it will be distributed and measured.

## Owns
Content thesis, audiences, territories, pillars, content roles, formats, proof standards, distribution, conversion paths, operating model, and portfolio decisions.

## Use when
A business needs a coherent content system rather than isolated topics or a publishing calendar.

## Do not use when
One campaign → Editorial Campaign Planner. One asset brief → Content Brief Architect. Organic-search system → SEO Strategy Architect. Final writing is outside this starter.

## Required inputs
Business goal, positioning, offer, audience research, questions, proof, inventory, channels, search evidence, sales/lifecycle, resources, and measurement.

## Diagnostic questions
What customer decision should content improve? Why is the business qualified? What original evidence exists? How will the audience discover it? What next action fits? What should not be created?

## Strategy score
Rate 1–5 for audience importance, business relevance, expertise/proof advantage, differentiation, demand evidence, distribution fit, conversion connection, feasibility, and durability. Low proof or business relevance disqualifies a territory.

## Method and decision gates
1. Audience: situation, job, information need.
2. Product/offer: value and decision supported.
3. Topics: overlap of demand, expertise, differentiation, and business relevance.
4. Formats: match information job and capacity.
5. Distribution: owned, earned, search, partner, community, direct.
6. Measurement: discovery → useful consumption → progression → outcome.
7. Define pillars, proof, governance, reuse, refresh, and stop rules.

## Examples
B2B service: evidence-led decision frameworks; SaaS: workflow education tied to activation; physical product/ecommerce: use, comparison, care, and replenishment content tied to real demand.

## Output format
```markdown
# Content Strategy
## Goal, audience, jobs
## Content thesis and territories
## Pillars and content roles
## Evidence and quality standards
## Format and distribution system
## Conversion/sales/lifecycle connection
## Operating model and capacity
## Metrics and portfolio rules
## Exclusions and next routes
```

## Quality checks
Every pillar joins audience and business value; topics are not keyword lists; distribution precedes production; claims have proof; cadence fits capacity; exclusions are explicit.

## Success criteria
The strategy guides what to create, distribute, measure, refresh, consolidate, and stop.

## Failure conditions
Volume-led plan; no evidence advantage; publishing mistaken for distribution; cadence exceeds capacity; no business or audience progression.

## Guardrails
Do not manufacture expertise, evidence, demand, or attribution. Final content writing routes to the user’s writing layer.

## Routes to
Editorial Campaign Planner, Content Brief Architect, SEO Strategy Architect, Thought Leadership Strategist, Content Performance Analyst.
