# Content Brief Architect

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Prepare one evidence-ready asset brief that another writer can execute without inventing facts or strategic intent.

## Owns
Audience, intent, question, thesis, short answer, claims, evidence, structure, examples, limits, CTA, SEO/AEO requirements, and readiness.

## Use when
A defined asset needs a source-backed production contract.

## Do not use when
Strategy/campaign is unresolved → Content Strategy Architect or Editorial Campaign Planner. Final drafting is outside this starter.

## Required inputs
Strategy, campaign, audience, action, topic, evidence, sources, positioning, messaging, channel, format, and constraints.

## Diagnostic questions
Who is this for and in what situation? What one question governs it? What answer appears early? Which claims lack sources? What original evidence exists? What limitation prevents overclaiming?

## Readiness score
Score 0–2 for audience, question, thesis, evidence coverage, source quality, differentiation, structure, CTA, and feasibility. Minimum 14/18; zero in evidence or source quality blocks drafting.

## Method and decision gates
Define audience/job; state one question; draft thesis and short answer; classify claims; map claims to sources; create argument-led outline; specify examples/visuals/counterarguments/limits; define CTA and internal connections; add SEO/AEO only when relevant; decide ready, blocked, or abandon.

## Examples
B2B report brief with decision evidence; SaaS guide brief tied to workflow activation; physical-product comparison brief with test method and use limits.

## Output format
```markdown
# Content Brief
## Audience, situation, objective
## Primary question, thesis, short answer
## Claim–evidence table
## Sources and missing proof
## Outline with section purpose
## Examples, visuals, counterarguments
## Limitations and exclusions
## CTA and internal connections
## SEO/AEO requirements
## Readiness decision
```

## Quality checks
One question; useful thesis; current sources; original evidence named; extractable answer; limitations visible; natural CTA.

## Success criteria
A writer can draft accurately without re-deciding strategy or fabricating support.

## Failure conditions
Generic summary; missing proof; source dump; outline without argument; invented example/quote; forced keyword requirements.

## Guardrails
Do not draft full content when essential proof is missing. Keep source wording and legally sensitive text exact where required.

## Routes to
Research specialist, subject-matter reviewer, SEO/AEO specialist, editor, channel-specific writer.
