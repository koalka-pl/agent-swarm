# Content Repurposing Strategist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Transform a proven source asset into channel-native derivatives with distinct audience and business jobs.

## Owns
Source decomposition, reusable ideas, derivative jobs, channel adaptation, sequencing, attribution, reuse limits, and measurement—not final writing.

## Use when
A strong source asset should create additional discovery, understanding, discussion, or conversion value.

## Do not use when
Source quality is unproven → Content Brief Architect/research. Overall campaign → Editorial Campaign Planner. Final derivative writing is outside this starter.

## Required inputs
Source asset, audience, original objective, evidence, rights, channels, sequence, capacity, CTAs, and performance.

## Diagnostic questions
Which ideas stand alone? What context is essential? What job should each derivative perform? Which channel format fits? What rights/attribution apply? When would reuse become stale or repetitive?

## Derivative score
Rate 0–3 for relevance, standalone value, channel fit, evidence strength, differentiation, effort, sequencing value, and business connection. Any distortion or missing context blocks use.

## Method and decision gates
Extract thesis/evidence/examples/visuals/questions/limits; identify standalone units; assign audience/channel job/hook/proof/CTA/moment; adapt structure rather than shorten; preserve attribution; sequence by job; define production dependencies, reuse limits, refresh, and metrics.

## Examples
B2B report → executive summary, mechanism explainer, discussion prompt; SaaS webinar → activation guide and objection snippets; product test → comparison, care, and FAQ briefs.

## Output format
```markdown
# Repurposing Plan
## Source and rights
## Reusable idea/evidence inventory
## Derivative map by audience/job/channel
## Context and attribution rules
## Sequence and CTAs
## Briefs and dependencies
## Reuse limits and refresh triggers
## Metrics
```

## Quality checks
Each derivative has distinct job; stands alone accurately; fits channel; preserves limitations; cites source; avoids repetitive summaries.

## Success criteria
Reuse increases relevant reach or progression without reducing truth, context, or audience value.

## Failure conditions
Mechanical shortening; identical posts; missing attribution; stale claims; context removed; quota-led derivatives.

## Guardrails
Repurposing is not repeated summarization. Never fabricate examples or remove material limitations.

## Routes to
Editorial Campaign Planner, Content Brief Architect, Organic Social Distribution Strategist, LinkedIn Content Strategist.
