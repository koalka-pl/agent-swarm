# Editorial Campaign Planner

**Version:** 1.0  
**Maturity:** full skill

## Purpose
Turn content strategy into a sequenced, capacity-aware campaign that builds a coherent argument and supports one measurable objective.

## Owns
Campaign objective, audience progression, narrative arc, anchor asset, sequence, briefs, dependencies, owners, statuses, distribution, reviews, and end decision.

## Use when
A bounded multi-asset campaign must turn strategy into an executable sequence.

## Do not use when
Overall content strategy → Content Strategy Architect. One brief → Content Brief Architect. Final writing is outside this starter.

## Required inputs
Strategy, objective, audience, offer/action, duration, evidence, channels, capacity, dates, contributors, approvals, and metrics.

## Diagnostic questions
What one outcome matters? What must the audience understand next? What evidence is available before drafting? Which asset anchors the sequence? Can capacity sustain cadence? What happens if an item slips?

## Campaign score
Rate 1–5 for objective clarity, relevance, coherence, evidence, business connection, distribution, capacity, reuse, and measurability. Below 3 on evidence/capacity requires scope reduction.

## Method and decision gates
Define objective and progression; choose bounded thesis; inventory evidence; design problem → stakes → mechanism → proof → alternatives/limits → action; select anchor/supporting jobs; sequence dependencies/distribution; assign owner/date/status/proof/CTA; add evidence/editorial/approval gates; define weekly and final decisions.

## Examples
B2B: six-week authority campaign; SaaS: adoption campaign around one workflow; ecommerce: seasonal use-case campaign constrained by inventory and fulfillment.

## Output format
```markdown
# Editorial Campaign
## Objective and audience progression
## Thesis and narrative arc
## Evidence inventory
## Anchor and supporting asset jobs
## Calendar, owners, dependencies
## Distribution and CTA logic
## Review/status gates
## Metrics, thresholds, end decision
```

## Quality checks
Each asset has a distinct job; evidence precedes drafting; sequence accumulates understanding; cadence fits capacity; CTAs fit readiness; one missed item does not collapse the system.

## Success criteria
The campaign creates coherent audience progression and decision-quality learning within available capacity.

## Failure conditions
Calendar without strategy; filler; unsupported claims; identical derivatives; vanity-only metrics; no end decision.

## Guardrails
Reduce scope before lowering evidence quality. Do not confuse publication count with progress.

## Routes to
Content Brief Architect, Content Repurposing Strategist, Organic Social Distribution Strategist, Content Performance Analyst.
